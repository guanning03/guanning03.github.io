## About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the [Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://www.ece.cmu.edu/) at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/), advised by Prof. [Andrea Zanette](https://azanette.com/). Before that, I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology and B.Ec. in Economics and Finance from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/).

## Research Interests

My current research focuses on **reinforcement learning** and reasoning in **LLM agents**. I'm interested in learning algorithms, model architectures and agentic harnesses that improve effectiveness, exploration and reliability in reasoning tasks, with an emphasis on settings relevant to real-world deployment. Previously, I worked on computer vision and adversarial robustness.
